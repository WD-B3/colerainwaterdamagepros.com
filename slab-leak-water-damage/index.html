Floor warm underfoot, or a water bill that jumped?

Those two signs together point to a slab leak, which is a pressurized line leaking under or inside a concrete floor. The trade name matters less than the symptoms. Other clues include the sound of running water when everything is off, low water pressure, damp carpet over a slab and a musty smell with no visible source.

If you see these signs in Colerain, OH, close the main shutoff and call (740) 231-9132. The water damage to flooring and walls is the part this service handles.

We do not locate or repair the leaking line. We do remove the water it spread and dry the building afterward.

When a slab leak is an emergency

Shut the water off now

If you suspect a slab leak, close the main shutoff to stop the loss and keep the leak from getting worse. Do not run water to test. Stay out of any room where water reaches outlets, and call the utility or 911 for electrical or gas concerns first.

Water that leaks beneath a slab can wash away soil and undermine the floor if it runs long enough. A slab leak you catch quickly is far less likely to cause lasting problems.

Slab leak repair options, in plain terms

A plumber usually proposes one of three paths. A spot repair breaks the slab at the leak and patches the pipe. Rerouting abandons the leaking line and runs a new one through the walls or attic. Repiping replaces the failing lines throughout.

Each has trade-offs in cost, mess and long-term reliability. Get the plumber's reasoning in writing, and tell the plumber a restoration crew is drying the area so both trades can coordinate.

Drying a floor that sits on a slab

Concrete holds moisture for a long time. Carpet and pad are usually lifted, laminate or vinyl is removed so the slab can dry and baseboards may be cut free. Hardwood laid over a slab may need extra care, which is covered on the hardwood floor page.

Dehumidification does most of the work on slabs. See structural drying for how readings guide the equipment.

Insurance and slab leaks

Policies vary on whether a leak under the slab is covered and whether the cost of reaching the pipe is included. Many cover resulting water damage but not the repair of the pipe itself. Ask your insurer before you hire anyone, and keep the plumber's written report.

How common slab leaks are here

Slab-on-grade floors exist in homes, additions, garages and some commercial buildings. We do not state how many Colerain homes sit on slabs because no primary source we can cite gives that figure. If you do have a slab, the mechanism applies. Ask the county or your plumber about your building.

Standards behind this page

Water from a pressurized supply line is Category 1 under ANSI/IICRC S500, but a slab holds moisture for a long time. Concrete is dried with dehumidification and readings, not with a short visit.

FAQ

Questions about slab leaks

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Colerain?

Yes. When a pressurized line is leaking, time matters, and the dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Can you find the leak?

Locating the line is a plumber's job with specialized tools. Once the plumber finds it, we handle the water and the drying.

Is a slab leak covered by homeowners insurance?

Sometimes. Coverage depends on the policy and on whether the damage was sudden. Check the language and ask your insurer.

Can I keep using water while I wait?

Close the main if you suspect a leak, and use the home as little as you can until a plumber has found it.

Will my carpet be ruined?

Carpet that sat on a wet slab is usually removed so the slab can dry, and pad is typically discarded. Fast action can save the carpet itself. The carpet page explains the decision.

A ceiling stain is the part of the problem you can see. Behind it, water sits in drywall, insulation and framing, and gravity keeps pulling it down. Ceiling water damage repair in Colerain, OH starts by finding where the water comes from, which may be a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an attic air handler. Call (740) 231-9132 any hour for the emergency line.

When a ceiling is an emergency

Call right away if the ceiling is dripping, sagging, bubbling or has a dark ring that is growing. Drywall that holds water gets heavy, and a sagging section can fall without warning.

Stay out from under a bulging ceiling

Move people, pets and furniture out of the room below. Do not poke at the sag with your hand. If water is near a light fixture or ceiling fan, leave the switch alone and turn off the circuit at the panel only if you can reach it dry.

If a bulge is filling with water, a small hole at its lowest point lets it drain into a bucket, but only do this when you are certain nothing electrical is above and you can stay clear of the debris.

Roof leaks that end up in the ceiling

A roof leak rarely appears directly below the hole. Water enters through damaged shingles, flashing or a vent boot, runs along the decking or a rafter and drips where it finds a low point, sometimes several feet away. That is why you can chase a stain for months without finding the roof defect.

This page owns roof-leak water damage inside the building. Crews dry the insulation and ceiling and repair the interior, while a roofer fixes the roof itself. Where snow melts and refreezes at the eaves, ice dams can push meltwater under shingles, which is worth a roofer's look in winter.

If you have already tarped or patched the roof, say so on the call. Interior drying should not wait for the weather to clear.

How a wet ceiling is dried and repaired

Crews measure the moisture, remove drywall that has sagged or stayed saturated, take out wet insulation and dry the framing. Then new drywall, finish coat and a stain-blocking primer go on, followed by paint.

Painting over a stain without drying only hides it. Brown rings come back through ordinary paint when the material underneath is still damp.

Insurance and ceiling damage

Sudden leaks, such as a storm that tears shingles or a pipe that bursts above the ceiling, are often covered. Wear and tear on an old roof is often not. Photograph the damage and the roof if you can, and ask your insurer how it treats both interior damage and the roof.

Related problems upstairs

If the water came from a failed pipe, read about the burst pipe cleanup. If it ran down inside the wall as well, the wall and drywall page covers that part. After a heavy storm, the storm damage page explains the full envelope.

Standards behind this page

ANSI/IICRC S500 classes water that comes from above, such as a roof leak running down through ceilings and walls, as a higher class because more materials get wet. That is why crews check the attic, the wall cavities and the floor below as well as the visible stain.

Safety steps before the crew arrives

  • Stay out from under a bulging or sagging ceiling.
  • Put a bucket under active drips and move furniture away.
  • Do not touch a wet light fixture. Turn off the circuit if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Do not poke a hole in a bulging ceiling unless a professional tells you to.
FAQ

Questions about ceiling water damage

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Colerain?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. A dripping or sagging ceiling is an emergency, and the dispatcher will tell you how to keep people clear while you wait.

Do you repair the roof?

Roof repair is a roofer's work. This service handles the interior: drying, removing wet material and repairing the ceiling. Tell the dispatcher about the roof and the crew plans around it.

Can I just paint over a ceiling stain?

Only after the material is dry and the source is fixed. Paint over a damp stain traps moisture and the ring comes back.

Should I pop a sagging ceiling to drain it?

Only when nothing electrical is above, you can stand clear and a bucket is ready. Many people prefer to leave it for the crew. If in doubt, stay out of the room and call.

How do I find where the leak starts?

The assessment traces moisture with meters and thermal imaging. For a roof leak, a roofer inspects the exterior, and the two trades work in parallel.

Bridgeport lies across the Ohio River from Wheeling, West Virginia, at the mouth of Wheeling Creek. About 1,582 people lived in the village at the 2020 census. Water damage restoration in Bridgeport, OH comes from Colerain Township, about 4 miles northwest of town as the crow flies. Call (740) 231-9132 if water is inside a home or shop.

Where a creek meets a river

The village sits at about 660 feet, low among Belmont County towns, where Wheeling Creek runs into the Ohio. Water has a long history here. When the creek rises after hard rain, it backs against the river, and nearby streets and basements get the runoff.

Wheeling Creek also flows through Blaine and Lansing in Colerain Township, so a storm upstream can send water downstream toward Bridgeport. After the April 2024 floods, Belmont County reported about $15.7 million in preliminary public infrastructure damage.

A village with two river crossings

Bridgeport is joined to Wheeling by the Military Order of the Purple Heart Bridge and part of the Fort Henry Bridge. Two older crossings to Wheeling Island, the Aetnaville and Bridgeport bridges, are closed or demolished. Colonel Ebenezer Zane laid the town out in 1806 as Canton. Its population peaked at 4,853 in 1940. Many houses are from that era or earlier, and old stock means old plumbing.

Insurance after creek flooding

Homeowners policies typically exclude flood, which the Ohio Department of Insurance notes must be bought separately and usually has a 30-day waiting period. Even one inch of floodwater can cause $25,000 of damage, the department says. If you live near the creek mouth, check your policy and ask Belmont County's floodplain administrator whether your lot is in a mapped flood area.

What happens after the creek goes down

Creek flooding leaves silt, debris and contaminated water behind long after the level drops. Mud packs into basements, behind baseboards and under flooring, and it dries into a crust that is hard to remove and carries bacteria. Wet drywall and insulation that sat in creek water are usually removed, while hard surfaces are washed and sanitized.

Do not let the mud dry in place. Tell the dispatcher how deep the water got and how long it stood, and photograph the line on the walls. If you rent, notify the landlord the same day. Keep children and pets out of the area until it has been cleaned, since floodwater can carry sewage and chemicals.

Small-village homes and rentals

Many Bridgeport houses are single-family homes on small lots, and a good number are rentals. Rental water damage involves a landlord, a tenant and sometimes two insurers. Tell the dispatcher who holds the lease and who handles the insurance claim, and the scope is written so each side can use it.

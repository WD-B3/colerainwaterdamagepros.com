What water damage restoration means in Colerain, OH

Water damage restoration is the work of removing water from a building, drying the structure, cleaning what can be saved and repairing what cannot. In Colerain, OH, it starts with a call to the 24/7 dispatch line at (740) 231-9132 and a plain description of what is wet.

This site covers Colerain Township in Belmont County, northwest of Wheeling, West Virginia. It is not the Colerain Township near Cincinnati. The township was organized in 1808, covers about 25 square miles and had 4,174 residents in the 2020 census, and parts of Blaine and Lansing sit inside its lines.

The calls that matter most are the urgent ones: a ceiling dripping after a roof leak, a supply line that let go under a sink, a basement taking on water, a drain pushing sewage back up. Each has its own first steps, and each has its own page below. This page covers the whole job from first call to finished repair.

Emergency steps

The first 24 to 72 hours after water gets in

Time decides how large a water loss becomes. The EPA advises drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, so the first day is the one that counts. Water also weighs more than most people expect, since one inch across a 1,000 square foot floor is about 623 gallons and more than 5,000 pounds.

  1. Stop the source if you safely can. Close the fixture valve or the main water shutoff.
  2. Stay out of any room where water touches outlets, panels or appliances. If the panel is dry and reachable, turn off power to the wet area, and call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas concern first.
  3. Photograph and video every wet surface, the source and damaged belongings before you move anything.
  4. Lift furniture legs off wet carpet, move valuables to dry rooms and keep children and pets out of standing water.
  5. Call the dispatch line and describe what you see. The assessment that follows sets the scope, and the price is explained before work starts.

The next day or two is when materials are removed that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated carpet pad, and when air movers and dehumidifiers start pulling moisture out of framing and subfloors. Crews track progress with moisture meters and thermal imaging until readings reach dry standards, which is the point of the structural drying stage.

Appliances and HVAC

Local conditions

What water does in and around Colerain Township

Wheeling Creek runs through Blaine and Lansing, both partly inside the township. Creek country means runoff can rise quickly after hard rain, so keep your shutoff locations known and your valuables off basement floors before a storm.

Belmont County's worst flood on record in the public documents is the flash flood of June 14 and 15, 1990 on Pipe Creek and Wegee Creek near Shadyside. The U.S. Geological Survey reported at least 24 deaths and more than $10 million in property damage, with a recurrence interval above 100 years on Pipe Creek. It is a reminder that creek floods here can arrive faster than a warning.

More recently, Belmont County's emergency management director estimated about $15.7 million in preliminary public infrastructure damage from flooding in April 2024, as reported by The Times Leader. After a wet spring, check basements and crawl spaces for dampness that rain left behind.

If you plan to rebuild in a mapped flood hazard area, the county floodplain administrator works through the Belmont County Commissioners' office at 101 West Main Street in Saint Clairsville. Ask before you build, because a flood hazard area permit may apply.

Older buildings add their own wrinkle. Structures from the 1800s, like the Concord Hicksite Friends Meeting House built in 1815, remind us that plaster, masonry and heavy timber hold moisture longer than modern drywall. In an older building, drying should be measured with a meter, not judged by touch.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they call

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Colerain?

Yes. The dispatch line at (740) 231-9132 is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays. Tell the dispatcher what is wet, where the water is coming from and whether you have been able to shut it off, and the call goes from there.

How quickly can a crew get to my property?

We do not promise an arrival window, because roads, weather and the number of active jobs change it every day. What we can say is that same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly what to expect on your call.

While you wait, stopping the water at its source matters more than anything else. The main shutoff is usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter or in the basement.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Often it does when the loss is sudden and accidental, such as a pipe that bursts or a water heater that fails. Slow leaks and neglected maintenance are often excluded, and flood damage is usually excluded unless you carry separate flood coverage. Read your policy or ask your insurer before you decide anything, and keep photos of everything.

Is water damage restoration the same as waterproofing a basement?

No. Restoration deals with the water that already got in: removal, drying, cleaning and repair. Waterproofing changes grading, drainage or foundation walls so water stays out in the future, and it is a different trade. If your basement takes on water every storm, fix the cause as well as the damage.

Can I rent air movers and dehumidifiers instead?

Equipment rental is not part of this service. Crews place and monitor the equipment because where each air mover sits and what the moisture readings say decide how long drying takes. Machines running in the wrong spots dry a room slowly and leave wet framing behind.

What if the water has been there for days, like in a vacant house?

Call anyway and say how long it has been. A long-running leak in an empty house often means wet insulation, soaked subfloors and a larger scope, and your insurer will want dated photos and a record of when you found it. The dispatcher can explain what an assessment covers before anyone drives out.

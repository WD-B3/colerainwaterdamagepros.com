Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website and phone line collect. It is written in plain language and applies to colerainwaterdamagepros.com.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or comment sections. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site.

Phone calls and email

When you call (740) 231-9132, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell the dispatcher, such as your name, address and a description of the damage. We use it to send a crew and to follow up on the job. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send, and we use it to reply.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser information when a page loads, under Google's own privacy policy. This site does not run advertising or tracking scripts of its own.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the people who need it to do the job, such as the crew on site, and where the law requires it. If you ask us to share documentation with your insurer, we will.

Retention and questions

We keep job records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and insurance requirements. To ask about your information, email contact@colerainwaterdamagepros.com.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Colerain and nearby towns we cover

Dispatch covers Colerain Township and the towns around it in Belmont County and nearby Mount Pleasant in Jefferson County.

Carpet squishes underfoot or smells musty?

Both mean the pad underneath is holding water. Carpet dries quickly on the surface but the pad acts like a sponge, and moisture sits against the subfloor. Carpet water damage in Colerain, OH starts with one question, which is whether the water was clean, gray or contaminated. Call (740) 231-9132 and describe the source.

Save it or replace it?

SituationUsual outcome
Clean water, wet less than 48 hours.Carpet is often saved with extraction and drying, and the pad is checked.
Clean water, wet several days.Carpet may survive, but pad is often replaced and odor risk rises.
Gray water, such as a washer drain.Cleaning and sanitizing, or replacement, depending on duration.
Sewage or flood water.Carpet and pad are usually discarded. See sewage backup cleanup.

Pad is cheap relative to carpet, so crews often replace pad even when the carpet is saved.

What to do before the crew arrives

  1. Stop the source of water.
  2. Remove furniture or put blocks or foil under legs to protect the carpet.
  3. Do not walk on wet carpet more than necessary, and keep pets off.
  4. Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  5. Open windows if the weather is dry, and run a fan if the outlet is dry.

How wet carpet is dried

Extraction pulls the bulk of the water out with a powerful unit. The carpet is lifted and pad is removed or the carpet is floated over the pad on special equipment that dries the pad in place. Air movers and dehumidifiers finish the job, and meters check the subfloor and the carpet backing.

The structural drying page explains the equipment. On slab floors, see the slab leak page for hidden sources of moisture.

Odor and stains

A musty smell means moisture or bacteria in the pad or backing. Cleaning and drying usually removes odor from clean-water losses, while some sewage or old stains need replacement. Dye in rugs or furniture can bleed into wet carpet, so lift items off quickly.

If the smell returns after drying, ask for another meter reading. It may mean damp subfloor or wall bottoms that still hold moisture.

Insurance and carpet

Insurers may pay for pad replacement and carpet cleaning after a covered leak and may require drying records. Photograph the carpet and keep a swatch if it is discarded. Ask your insurer how it handles matching in rooms that share a carpet.

Carpeted basements and family rooms are common in the area, and basements are the first place storm water goes. See the basement page for what happens when the carpet is below grade.

Why the pad matters more than the carpet

Carpet fibers are mostly synthetic and dry on their own. The pad beneath is foam or fiber that absorbs several times its weight in water, releases it slowly and can harbor odor for months. A room can feel dry on top while the pad still sits wet against the subfloor.

That is why a meter reading under the carpet matters. Crews lift a corner, check the pad and subfloor and decide whether drying in place will work. If the pad stays damp after a few days, it comes out and a new one goes in, which is a small cost compared with a recurring smell.

Stairs, closets and edges

Carpet on stairs and in closets is easy to overlook. Water follows tack strips and baseboards, and edges stay wet when the center dries. Ask the crew to check along walls, in closets and at doorways, where moisture lingers and mold likes to start.

If a leak came from upstairs, water often runs down walls and soaks carpet in the room below. Describe both rooms when you call.

Standards behind this page

Whether carpet is saved depends on the water category defined in ANSI/IICRC S500 and on how long it stayed wet. The EPA's mold guidance says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit growth, which is the window crews work against.

FAQ

Questions about carpet water damage

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and drying started quickly. Contaminated water means replacement.

Do you replace carpet pad?

Pad is often replaced even when carpet is saved, because it holds water and odor.

Is a wet carpet dangerous?

It can grow mold if left wet and may harbor bacteria if the water was dirty. Dry it quickly and keep children and pets away from contaminated areas.

How long does carpet take to dry?

A few days with equipment for most clean-water losses. Readings, not the calendar, show when it is done.

Will you clean the carpet afterward?

Cleaning is part of the plan when carpet is saved. Ask what method will be used for your carpet type.

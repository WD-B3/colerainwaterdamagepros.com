Every kind of water damage, one phone number

Water gets into a building in many ways, and each way leaves a different kind of damage. This index groups the nineteen services by cause so you can find the page closest to what you are looking at. If you are not sure, call (740) 231-9132 and describe it. The dispatcher will point you to the right crew.

Group 1 of 6

Pipes and plumbing

Supply lines fail in several ways, and the damage depends on how long the water ran. A burst pipe floods a room within minutes, a frozen pipe splits in cold snaps and floods when it thaws, a leaking water heater soaks the floor around it and a slab leak sends water up through a concrete floor.

  • Burst Pipe Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
  • Frozen Pipe Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
  • Water Heater Leak Cleanup (emergency, 24/7)
  • Slab Leak Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
Group 2 of 6

Appliances and HVAC

Appliances and cooling equipment cause many small leaks that grow. A failed hose is covered under appliance leak damage, a clogged condensate line is covered under AC leak damage and a sink, dishwasher or ice line leak is covered under kitchen water damage.

  • Appliance Leak Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
  • AC Leak Water Damage
  • Kitchen Water Damage
Group 3 of 6

Weather and building envelope

Rain, runoff and creek water find their way in through roofs, walls and foundations. A ceiling water damage page covers roof leaks and sagging ceilings, storm water damage covers wind-driven rain, flood damage covers rising water, basement water damage covers the lowest room and crawl space water damage covers the void beneath the floor.

  • Ceiling Water Damage Repair (emergency, 24/7)
  • Storm Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
  • Flood Damage Cleanup (emergency, 24/7)
  • Basement Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)
  • Crawl Space Water Damage
Group 4 of 6

Water removal and drying

These services describe the work itself. Water extraction removes standing water, structural drying and dehumidification dries the building and sewage backup cleanup handles contaminated water with protective gear.

  • Water Extraction (emergency, 24/7)
  • Structural Drying
  • Sewage Backup Cleanup (emergency, 24/7)
Group 5 of 6

Floors and walls

Floors and walls take the visible damage. See hardwood floor water damage for boards that cup or buckle, carpet water damage for wet carpet and pad and wall and drywall water damage for stained or soft walls.

  • Hardwood Floor Water Damage
  • Carpet Water Damage
  • Wall and Drywall Water Damage
Group 6 of 6

Commercial

Businesses face downtime, inventory and tenants on top of the water. Commercial water damage restoration describes staged drying that keeps parts of a building open.

  • Commercial Water Damage (emergency, 24/7)

What to do first

Whatever the cause, the first minutes follow the same order. Stop the water if you can, stay clear of electrical hazards, photograph the damage and call.

  • Close the nearest shutoff or the main.
  • Do not enter rooms where water reaches outlets or the panel.
  • Move small valuables off wet floors.
  • Do not use household fans or heaters to force things dry.
